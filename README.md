# Selected Writing

A collection of essays on philosophy, disability, ethics, family, and personal experience.

My writing often explores a question that also shapes my engineering work: **how do the systems, categories, and environments we create affect the way people experience the world?**

---

## Philosophy & Ethics

### [Judgment as a Condition of Objectivity: Disability and Biomedical Classification](02-objectivity-disability-classification.pdf)

An examination of scientific judgment, evidential sufficiency, and objectivity through the case of disability classification.

`Philosophy of Science` `Disability` `Scientific Objectivity`

### [What About Me? Ethical Blind Spots in Prenatal Screening: A Sibling's Perspective](05-prenatal-screening-siblings-perspective.pdf)

A bioethical examination of prenatal screening that expands the discussion of parental autonomy and disability to include the often-overlooked perspective of siblings.

`Bioethics` `Disability Ethics` `Family`

## Disability Justice & Accessibility

### [The Cycle of Exclusion](03-cycle-of-exclusion.pdf)

An analysis of the gap between institutional claims of inclusion and the lived usability of disability accommodations, using accessibility at Princeton as a case study.

`Disability Justice` `Accessibility` `Institutional Inclusion`

## Personal Essays

### [I Love You Too](01-i-love-you-too.pdf)

`Family` `Communication` `Disability`

### [I Am Sorry](04-i-am-sorry.pdf)

`Family` `Ableism` `Identity`

### [Beyond Words](06-beyond-words.pdf)

`Communication` `Family` `Empathy`

## Themes

Across these pieces, I return to questions about:

- disability and accessibility;
- ethics and scientific judgment;
- family and caregiving;
- communication and difference; and
- how institutions and social expectations shape individual experience.
